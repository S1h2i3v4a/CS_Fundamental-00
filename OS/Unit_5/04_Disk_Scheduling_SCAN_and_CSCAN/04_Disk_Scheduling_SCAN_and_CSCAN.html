1. Comparison: SCAN vs C-SCAN

ParameterSCAN (Elevator)C-SCAN (Circular SCAN)
Service DirectionBoth directions (Reverses at boundary)Strictly ONE direction only
Return PathServices requests on way backImmediate fly-back to 0 without service
Wait Time VarianceHigher variance (favors middle cylinders)Uniform wait time for all cylinders
Boundary TouchTouches 0 or MAX cylinderTouches both 0 and MAX cylinders
