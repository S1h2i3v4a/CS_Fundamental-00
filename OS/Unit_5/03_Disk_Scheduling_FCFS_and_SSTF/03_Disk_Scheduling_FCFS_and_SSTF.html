Module 03: Disk Scheduling — FCFS and SSTF

AKTU BCS401 Unit 5 Disk Arm Optimization

1. Algorithm Principles

  • FCFS: Pure FIFO order. Fair with no starvation, but causes wild head swings.
  • SSTF: Greedy local minimum search. Drastically reduces seek time, but causes Starvation.
