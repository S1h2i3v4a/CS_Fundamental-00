3. Solved Numerical: UNIX Inode Capacity Derivation

Problem: Block Size = 4 KB, Pointer = 4 B. Inode: 12 Direct, 1 Single, 1 Double, 1 Triple Indirect.
1. Pointers per block K = 4096 / 4 = 1024 pointers (210).
2. Direct = 12 × 4 KB = 48 KB.
3. Single Indirect = 1024 × 4 KB = 4 MB.
4. Double Indirect = (1024)2 × 4 KB = 4 GB.
5. Triple Indirect = (1024)3 × 4 KB = 4 TB.
Total Max File Size ≈ 4 TB.

4. Top Core Systems Interview Q&A

Q: Why do SSDs not use SCAN or Elevator scheduling?
A: SSDs use solid-state flash memory without mechanical moving heads or rotating platters. Rotational latency and seek time are 0. Every block has identical O(1) access latency. Hence, SCAN provides zero benefit and adds unnecessary scheduling overhead. Modern SSDs use NVMe FIFO / multi-queue schedulers.
