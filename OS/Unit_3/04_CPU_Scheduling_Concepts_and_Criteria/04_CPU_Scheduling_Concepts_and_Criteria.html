3. Preemptive vs Non-Preemptive Summary

Non-Preemptive: Process willingly CPU release karta hai (I/O or Terminate). RT == WT.
Preemptive: OS timer interrupt ya higher priority aane par forcibly CPU preempt kar sakta hai.
