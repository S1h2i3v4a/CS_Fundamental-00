9. Classical Problems: Dining Philosophers & Sleeping Barber

Syllabus Coverage: Gateway Classes Slides 93–107 & AKTU Syllabus | Circular Wait Deadlock, Asymmetric Solution, Barber Synchronization.

9.1 Dining Philosophers Deadlock Prevention

Deadlock arises when all philosophers simultaneously grab their left chopstick. Solved using asymmetric pickup or limiting diners to N-1.

9.2 Sleeping Barber Mechanics

  • customers = 0: Barber blocks on wait(customers) until customer wakes him.
  • barbers = 0: Customer blocks on wait(barbers) until barber chair is vacant.
  • mutex = 1: Protects waiting counter from concurrent updates.
