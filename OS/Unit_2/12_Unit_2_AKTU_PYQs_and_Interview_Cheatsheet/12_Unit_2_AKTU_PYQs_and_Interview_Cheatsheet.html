12.1 High-Yield Solved Exam Questions

  • Critical Section Criteria: Mutual Exclusion (Primary), Progress (Primary), Bounded Waiting (Secondary), Speed Neutrality.
  • Peterson's Algorithm: Proves mutual exclusion via single integer turn. Bounded wait $k=1$.
  • Overcoming Busy Waiting: Use block() and wakeup() queue-based semaphore implementation.
  • Producer-Consumer Trap: Always place counting semaphore (empty/full) before mutex to prevent deadlock.
  • Process Tree Formula: $n$ consecutive forks yield $2^n$ total processes and $2^n - 1$ child processes.
  • Zombie vs Orphan: Zombie = Dead child waiting for parent's wait(); Orphan = Running child adopted by PID 1.
