10.1 The fork() System Call

Clones the calling parent process into an identical child address space.

  • pid == 0 in Child process.
  • pid > 0 in Parent process (value is child PID).
  • pid == -1 on process creation failure.

10.2 Mathematical Process Tree Growth

Formula for $n$ consecutive fork() statements:

Total Processes = 2^n
Total Child Processes Created = 2^n - 1
