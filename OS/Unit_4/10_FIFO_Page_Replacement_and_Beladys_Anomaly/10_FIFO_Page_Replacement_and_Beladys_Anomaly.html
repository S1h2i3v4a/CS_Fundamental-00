Module 10: FIFO Page Replacement and Belady's Anomaly

AKTU BCS401 Unit 4 Page Replacement Paradox

1. FIFO Algorithm & Dirty Bit

FIFO memory me sabse pehle enter karne wale page ko replace karta hai. Dirty Bit (0/1) indicate karta hai ki page modify hua hai ya nahi; clean page evict karte samay disk write save ho jata hai.

2. Belady's Anomaly Proof

Normally frames badhane se faults kam hote hain, lekin FIFO me frames 3 se 4 karne par faults 9 se badhkar 10 ho jate hain!

3. Summary Comparison

Why it happens: FIFO is NOT a Stack Algorithm ($S_n ot\subseteq S_{n+1}$).
Stack Algorithms: LRU and Optimal algorithms strictly follow subset property and NEVER suffer from Belady's Anomaly!
