Module 14: Locality of Reference and Cache Organization

AKTU BCS401 Unit 4 Locality & Cache Hardware

1. Principle of Locality

  • Temporal Locality: Same location accessed repeatedly soon (Loops, counters).
  • Spatial Locality: Nearby consecutive memory locations accessed soon (Arrays, sequential instructions).
