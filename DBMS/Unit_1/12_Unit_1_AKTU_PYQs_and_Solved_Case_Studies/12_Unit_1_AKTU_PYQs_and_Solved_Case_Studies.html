3. Core Interview Q&A

Q: Why does Primary Key strictly prohibit NULL?
A: Entity Integrity Constraint demands that every tuple must have an unambiguous, non-null physical identity. In SQL three-valued logic, NULL = NULL evaluates to UNKNOWN, making two NULL keys mathematically indistinguishable.
