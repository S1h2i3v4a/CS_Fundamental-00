1. EER Architecture & Taxonomy

Disjoint (d): Entity can belong to at most ONE subclass (e.g. Can't be Manager AND Secretary simultaneously). • Overlapping (o): Entity can belong to MULTIPLE subclasses (e.g. Employee can be Teacher AND Student). 2. Completeness Constraint: • Total (Double line): Every superclass entity MUST belong to at least one subclass. • Partial (Single line): Some superclass entities may not belong to any subclass. Aggregation: Treating a relationship between entity sets as a higher-level composite entity so it can enter a secondary relationship.

3. Core EER Constraints

• Disjoint (d): Entity can belong to at most 1 subclass.
• Overlapping (o): Entity can simultaneously belong to multiple subclasses.
• Total (Double Line): Entity must belong to at least 1 subclass.
• Partial (Single Line): Entity may not belong to any defined subclass.
• Aggregation: Abstracts a relationship into a higher-level composite entity.
