Procedural Query Foundation: Relational Algebra specifies WHAT data to retrieve and the exact procedural STEP-BY-STEP pipeline.

1. Fundamental Operators

Fundamental operators are atomic. The 5 core operators are Selection (σ), Projection (π), Union (∪), Set Difference (-), and Cartesian Product (×).

OperatorSymbolDimensionKey BehaviorClosure Property
Selectionσp(R)Horizontal (Rows)Filters tuples based on predicate condition p.Preserves degree, reduces cardinality.
ProjectionπA(R)Vertical (Cols)Extracts specified columns and automatically eliminates duplicate rows.Reduces degree to |A|.
UnionR ∪ SRow ConcatenationCombines tuples. Requires Union Compatibility (same degree & domains).Removes duplicates.
Set DifferenceR - STuple SubtractionTuples present in R but absent in S. Non-commutative (R - S ≠ S - R).Union compatible.
Cartesian ProductR × SAll CombinationsPairs every tuple of R with every tuple of S. deg = m + n, card = m * n.No condition needed.

2. Derived Joins & Division

Natural Join (R ⋈ S) performs an equality join on common columns and drops redundant duplicate columns. Outer joins (Left, Right, Full) preserve non-matching dangling tuples by padding missing values with NULLs.

Division Operator (R ÷ S): Formulated as πA(R) - πA((πA(R) × S) - R). Used to answer "FOR ALL / EVERY" queries.

3. Solved AKTU Numerical Schema

Suppliers-Parts Database: SUPPLIERS(sid, sname, address), PARTS(pid, pname, color), CATALOG(sid, pid, cost).

-- Find names of suppliers who supply some RED part:
π[sname]( SUPPLIERS ⋈ π[sid]( CATALOG ⋈ σ[color = 'RED'](PARTS) ) )

-- Find suppliers who supply EVERY part:
π[sid, pid](CATALOG) ÷ π[pid](PARTS)
