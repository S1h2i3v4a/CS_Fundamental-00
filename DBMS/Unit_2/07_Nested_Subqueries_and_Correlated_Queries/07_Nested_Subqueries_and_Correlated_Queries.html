Module 07: Nested Subqueries and Correlated Queries

Nested vs Correlated: Independent subqueries execute once; correlated subqueries evaluate repeatedly for every candidate row of the outer query.

1. Multi-Row Operators

OperatorLogicEquivalence
INMatches any value in the returned list.= ANY
> ANYGreater than at least one value in the list.> MIN(...)
> ALLGreater than every single value in the list.> MAX(...)

2. EXISTS Short-Circuiting

EXISTS terminates immediately upon finding the first match, making it superior to IN when querying large correlated tables.
