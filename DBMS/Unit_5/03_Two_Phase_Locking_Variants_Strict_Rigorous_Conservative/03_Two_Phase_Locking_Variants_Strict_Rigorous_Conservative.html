1. Why 2PL Variants are Necessary

Basic 2PL guarantees Conflict Serializability, but allows Deadlocks and Cascading Aborts. Variants solve these operational bottlenecks.

2. Comparative Breakdown of Variants

VariantLock Release TimingDeadlock-Free?Cascadeless?
Basic 2PLShrinking phaseNoNo
Conservative 2PLPre-locks all items at startYESNo
Strict 2PLExclusive locks held till Commit/AbortNoYES (Strict)
Rigorous 2PLAll locks (S & X) held till Commit/AbortNoYES (Strict)
Industry Standard: Strict 2PL
Modern RDBMS (PostgreSQL, MySQL InnoDB, Oracle) employ Strict 2PL because it guarantees strict recoverability and simple before-image rollback while maintaining high read concurrency.
