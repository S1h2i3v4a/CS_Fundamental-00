1. Granularity Definition

Granularity refers to the size of data items chosen for locking (database, files, pages, records, fields).

2. Concurrency vs Overhead Trade-off

Fine Granularity (Record): Maximizes concurrent access, but incurs heavy lock table overhead.
Coarse Granularity (File/Database): Minimal locking overhead, but restricts concurrency.
