1. Comparative Metrics of Network Categories

Parameter PAN LAN MAN WAN
Geographic Range Within 10 meters Up to 1 - 2 km 5 to 50 km (City wide) Country, Continent, Global
Propagation Delay Negligible Very Short (Microseconds) Moderate (Milliseconds) High (Tens to hundreds of ms)
Error Rates Extremely Low Very Low (Shielded indoor) Moderate Highest (Atmospheric / Long haul)
Standard Protocol Bluetooth 802.15 Ethernet 802.3, Wi-Fi 802.11 DQDB, ATM, Metro Ethernet IP, BGP, MPLS, SD-WAN

2. 3-Tier ISP Ecosystem

  • Tier 1 ISPs: Global network giants operating trans-oceanic cables. They peer settlement-free with each other.
  • Tier 2 ISPs: Regional telecom operators who purchase transit from Tier 1 while peering laterally at IXPs.
  • Tier 3 ISPs: Last-mile retail broadband providers delivering bandwidth to homes and enterprises.
  • IXPs (Internet Exchange Points):** Physical switching centers where autonomous networks swap traffic locally to avoid expensive transit hops.
