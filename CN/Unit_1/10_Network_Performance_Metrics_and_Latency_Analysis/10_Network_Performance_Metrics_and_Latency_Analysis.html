1. Transmission Delay vs Propagation Delay (AKTU Favorite)

Parameter Transmission Delay ($T_t$) Propagation Delay ($T_p$)
Core Formula Tt = L / B Tp = d / v
Governing Factors Packet Size ($L$) & Link Bandwidth ($B$) Cable Distance ($d$) & Wave Speed ($v$)
Analogous Meaning Kitni der me pura packet car parking se nikla Car ko highway par chalne me kitna time laga
Independent Of Distance between nodes Packet size and transmission bit rate

2. Channel Parameter Ratio $a$

Data link layer protocols ki efficiency ratio $a$ par depend karti hai:

$$a = rac{T_p}{T_t} = rac{d \cdot B}{v \cdot L}$$

Agar $a \gg 1$ (High bandwidth, long distance jaise Satellite links), to Stop-and-Wait protocol extremely inefficient ho jata hai ($U pprox rac{1}{1 + 2a} o 0$).
