Module 08: Transmission Impairments & Signal Characteristics

AKTU BCS603 Focus: Attenuation metrics (dB calculations), Distortion in composite signals, 4 types of noise, and Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR).

1. Comparative Impairment Diagnostic Matrix

Impairment Primary Cause Affected Signals Engineering Remedy
Attenuation Medium resistance & thermal absorption over distance All Analog & Digital signals Amplifiers (Analog) & Repeaters (Digital)
Distortion Frequency-dependent propagation velocity (Phase shift) Composite signals only Equalizers & delay compensators
Thermal Noise Random electron agitation ($kTB$) All physical media Cooling; Shielding; Shannon capacity bounds
Crosstalk Mutual inductance between parallel copper wires Twisted pair, multi-core cables Differential signaling & tight wire twisting
Impulse Noise Lightning strikes, power grid switches, sparking Digital packets (Burst errors) Shielding (STP), Error correcting codes (CRC/FEC)
