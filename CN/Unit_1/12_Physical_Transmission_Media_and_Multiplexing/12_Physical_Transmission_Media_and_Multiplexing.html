Module 12: Physical Transmission Media & Multiplexing

AKTU BCS603 Focus: Guided media specs, Optical Fiber TIR physics, Wireless frequency propagation, and Multiplexing architectures (FDM, WDM, Synchronous vs Statistical TDM).

1. Single-Mode vs Multi-Mode Fiber Comparison

Feature Single-Mode Fiber (SMF) Multi-Mode Fiber (MMF)
Core Diameter 8 to 10 μm (Extremely thin) 50 to 62.5 μm (Wide core)
Light Source Laser Diodes (Infrared 1310/1550 nm) LEDs (Light Emitting Diodes 850 nm)
Modal Dispersion Zero (Single ray travel) High (Multiple rays bounce at different angles)
Distance Range Up to 100+ km without amplification Up to 2 km (Campus / Building backbones)

2. Synchronous TDM vs Statistical TDM

  • Synchronous TDM: Frame size is fixed. If host A is silent, slot A travels empty across the WAN. Efficiency drops when traffic is bursty.
  • Statistical TDM: Slots are dynamically addressed. Only stations with data in buffer are awarded slots. Requires addressing overhead in each slot, but maximizes throughput.
