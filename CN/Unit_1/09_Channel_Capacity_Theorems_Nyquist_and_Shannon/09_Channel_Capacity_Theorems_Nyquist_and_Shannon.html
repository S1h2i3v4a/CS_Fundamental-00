1. Summary Comparison: Nyquist vs Shannon

Dimension Nyquist Bit Rate Shannon Channel Capacity
Formula C = 2 · B · log₂(M) bps C = B · log₂(1 + SNR) bps
Channel Condition Ideal Noiseless Channel Realistic Thermal Noisy Channel

Physical Role Tells how many signal levels must be encoded Defines unbreakable speed ceiling set by nature

2. Solved Numerical: Combined Shannon & Nyquist

Question (AKTU Exam): Ek communication channel ki bandwidth $B = 1 ext{ MHz}$ hai aur uska SNR $63$ hai. (a) Shannon capacity nikalein. (b) Nyquist formula ke mutabiq is data rate ko achieve karne ke liye kitne signal levels ($M$) chahiye?

Solution:
(a) Shannon Capacity:
$$C = B \cdot \log_2(1 + 63) = 10^6 \cdot \log_2(64) = 10^6 imes 6 = 6 ext{ Mbps}$$
(b) Signal Levels ($M$):
$$C = 2 \cdot B \cdot \log_2(M) \implies 6 imes 10^6 = 2 imes 10^6 \cdot \log_2(M)$$
$$\log_2(M) = rac{6 imes 10^6}{2 imes 10^6} = 3 \implies M = 2^3 = 8 ext{ signal levels}$$
