Loss Recovery & Reno Dynamics

Congestion Signals Taxonomy, Severe RTO Timeout vs Mild 3 Duplicate ACKs, TCP Tahoe vs Reno Architectural Comparison, and Fast Recovery State Machine.

1. Severe vs Mild Congestion Response

RTO Timeout: ssthresh = cwnd / 2, cwnd = 1 MSS (Full reset to Slow Start)
3 Duplicate ACKs: Fast Retransmit missing segment immediately without waiting for RTO.
TCP Reno Fast Recovery: ssthresh = cwnd / 2, cwnd = ssthresh (Resumes Congestion Avoidance directly).
