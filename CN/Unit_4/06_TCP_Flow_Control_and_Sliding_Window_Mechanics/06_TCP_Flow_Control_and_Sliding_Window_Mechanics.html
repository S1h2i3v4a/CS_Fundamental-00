Credit-Based Byte-Oriented Sliding Window, Window Opening/Closing/Shrinking Dynamics, Zero-Window Deadlock Prevention via Persistence Timer, and Silly Window Syndrome.

1. Sliding Window Wall Dynamics

Window Size Equation: Effective Window = min(rwnd, cwnd)
Closing: Left wall moves right upon receiving ACKs for sent bytes.
Opening: Right wall moves right as receiver application drains buffer.
Shrinking: Right wall moves left (Forbidden / strongly discouraged by RFCs).
