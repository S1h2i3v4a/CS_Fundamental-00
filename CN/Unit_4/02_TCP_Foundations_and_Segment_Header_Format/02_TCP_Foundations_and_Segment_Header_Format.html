5 $\implies 5 imes 4 = 20 ext{ Bytes}$. Maximum HLEN = 15 $\implies 15 imes 4 = 60 ext{ Bytes}$. Sequence numbers consume 1 sequence count per SYN and FIN flag even if carrying 0 data bytes!

1. 14 Header Fields Summary

HLEN Equation: Header Length in Bytes = HLEN (4 bits) * 4 (Min = 20B, Max = 60B)
Control Flags (6 bits): URG | ACK | PSH | RST | SYN | FIN
Window Size (16 bits): rwnd buffer advertisement (Max standard = 65,535 Bytes)
