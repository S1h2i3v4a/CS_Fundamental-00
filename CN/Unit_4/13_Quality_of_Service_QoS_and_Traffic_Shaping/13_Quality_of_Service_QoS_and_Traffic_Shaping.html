1. Token Bucket Burst Mathematics

Burst Duration: T = C / (M - R)
Total Transmitted Data: D = M * T = C * M / (M - R)
Example: C = 1MB, M = 50MB/s, R = 10MB/s -> T = 1 / 40 = 25 milliseconds (1.25 MB total data)
