Unit 4: Transport Layer

Termination & State Dynamics

Four-Way Handshake with FIN & ACK, Half-Close Architecture, TIME-WAIT (2MSL) State Engineering, and Reset (RST) Flag Usage.

1. 4-Way Handshake Steps

StepDirectionFlagEquationsStates
1Client -> ServerFIN = 1Seq = xClient: FIN-WAIT-1
2Server -> ClientACK = 1Ack = x + 1Client: FIN-WAIT-2, Server: CLOSE-WAIT
3Server -> ClientFIN = 1Seq = yServer: LAST-ACK
4Client -> ServerACK = 1Ack = y + 1Server: CLOSED, Client: TIME-WAIT (2MSL)

2. TIME-WAIT (2MSL) Rationale

Reason 1: Allows retransmitting final ACK if Server's FIN times out.
Reason 2: Prevents old duplicate segments from corrupting future connections.
RST Flag: Abrupt teardown when connecting to closed port or during crash recovery.
