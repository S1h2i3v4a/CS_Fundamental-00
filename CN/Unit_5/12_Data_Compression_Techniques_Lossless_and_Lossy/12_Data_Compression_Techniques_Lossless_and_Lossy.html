1. Data Compression Paradigms

Compression maximizes network transmission throughput and minimizes mass storage consumption by eliminating data redundancy.

Lossless vs Lossy Segmentation
Lossless: Strict bit-for-bit mathematical identity restoration (RLE, Huffman, Lempel-Ziv).
Lossy: Psychoacoustic and visual human perception threshold pruning (MP3, JPEG, H.264).

2. Lossless Compression Schemes

AlgorithmOperational MethodTarget Data Stream
Run-Length Encoding (RLE)Substitutes contiguous identical byte runs with character-count pairsScanned monochrome documents, fax bitmaps
Huffman CodingBuilds minimum redundancy binary prefix trees based on symbol probabilitiesVariable-length character streams
Lempel-Ziv (LZW)Maintains dynamic dictionary table substituting recurrent words with pointersGeneral archive utilities (ZIP, GZIP, PNG)

3. Psychoacoustic Audio Masking Mechanics

  • Frequency Masking: A dominant louder sound tone suppresses softer tones residing within the critical spectral band.
  • Temporal Masking (Slide 88): Loud auditory impulses render the human cochlea unresponsive for several milliseconds immediately preceding and following the transient event.
