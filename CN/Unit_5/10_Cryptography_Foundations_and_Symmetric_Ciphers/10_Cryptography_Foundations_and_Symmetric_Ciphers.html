1. Cryptographic Principles & Security Dimensions

Cryptography ensures secure communication across four pillars: Confidentiality, Integrity, Authentication, and Non-repudiation.

Symmetric Cryptography Architecture
A single secret key K performs both encryption and decryption: C = E(K, P) and P = D(K, C). Requires pre-shared key establishment.

2. Classical Ciphers: Substitution vs Transposition

Cipher FamilyStructural MechanismVulnerability Profile
Substitution (Monoalphabetic)Replaces plaintext letters with static fixed alphabet mapping (e.g., Caesar shift)Vulnerable to natural letter frequency analysis (e.g., English 'E', 'T')
Transposition (Permutation)Shuffles letter positions without modifying underlying alphabet characters (Rail Fence, Columnar)Preserves original character frequency histograms
