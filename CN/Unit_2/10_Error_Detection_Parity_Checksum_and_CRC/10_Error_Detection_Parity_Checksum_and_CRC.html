Error Control & Polynomial Mathematics

Mathematical foundations of transmission error detection: 2D parity, 1's complement Internet checksum, Modulo-2 polynomial division (CRC), and error catching proofs.

1. CRC Modulo-2 Arithmetic

Cyclic Redundancy Check relies on polynomial algebra over Galois Field GF(2). Bit addition and subtraction are performed via carry-less XOR operations. Appending $r$ zeros followed by division yields the $r$-bit Frame Check Sequence (FCS).

CRC Operational Formula

Appended Message: M(x) · x^r
M(x) · x^r = Q(x) · G(x) + R(x)
Transmitted Codeword T(x) = M(x) · x^r + R(x)
Receiver Check: T(x) mod G(x) == 0 (Valid)
