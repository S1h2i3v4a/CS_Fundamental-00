2. Why Slotted ALOHA Outperforms Pure ALOHA

Pure ALOHA me agar koi dusri station kisi frame ke beech me bhi transmit kar de, to collision ho jata hai. Slotted ALOHA stations ko time boundaries par constrain karta hai: transmissions sirf slot start par ho sakti hain. Isse collision window aadhi ho jati hai ($2T_{fr} o T_{fr}$), aur channel throughput seedhe double ho jata hai!

AKTU Exam Shortcut: Agar question me Poochha jaye ki Pure ALOHA me $G=0.5$ par throughput kya hai: $S = 0.5 imes e^{-1} = 0.5 / 2.718 = 0.184$ (18.4%). Calculator par $e^{-x}$ function use karna na bhoolein!
