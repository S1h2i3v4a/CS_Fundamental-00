Module 03: Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA) & Persistence

AKTU BCS603 Focus: Carrier sensing principles, 1-Persistent vs Non-Persistent vs p-Persistent strategies, and Propagation Delay collision window analysis.

1. Comparative Strategy Flow

  • 1-Persistent: "Aggressive". Channel idle $ o$ Transmit immediately. Channel busy $ o$ Keep listening, transmit at first instant of idle. Pros: Zero wasted channel time. Cons: High collision when busy period ends.
  • Non-Persistent: "Polite". Channel idle $ o$ Transmit immediately. Channel busy $ o$ Wait random timer before re-sensing. Pros: Collision probability drops drastically. Cons: Channel sits idle while stations are in backoff sleep.
  • p-Persistent: "Optimal Compromise". Slotted channel. If idle, transmit with probability $p$. With $(1-p)$, defer to next slot. Balances throughput and collision rate.
