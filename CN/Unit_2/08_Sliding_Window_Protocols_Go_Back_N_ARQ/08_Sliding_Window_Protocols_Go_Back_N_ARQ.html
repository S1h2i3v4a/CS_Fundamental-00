1. Pipelining & Go-Back-N Mechanics

Pipelining overcomes the single-frame waiting penalty of Stop-and-Wait by maintaining a window of outstanding unacknowledged frames ($W_S$). Receiver window is strictly $W_R = 1$, enforcing sequential in-order acceptance without out-of-order buffering.

2. Formal Proof of $W_S = 2^m - 1$

If $W_S = 2^m$, a complete loss of an entire burst of ACKs forces retransmissions of old frames that wrap around modulo $2^m$, making it mathematically impossible for the receiver to distinguish duplicate retransmitted frames from brand new frames of the next cycle.

Go-Back-N Mathematical Governing Equations

Sender Window: W_S = 2^m - 1 | Receiver Window: W_R = 1
Efficiency: η = W_S / (1 + 2a) [for W_S < 1 + 2a]
Optimal Window for 100% η: W_S ≥ 1 + 2a
